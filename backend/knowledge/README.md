# Health Saathi Knowledge Base & Clinical References

This directory contains grounded nutrition, biomarker, and health education datasets for **Health Saathi (PCOS Saathi)**.

## Contents

- `indian_food_database.json`: Curated nutritional database of 10 regionally authentic Indian meals and snacks tailored for insulin sensitivity and PCOS lifestyle education.
  - Sourced from ICMR - National Institute of Nutrition (NIN) Dietary Guidelines for Indians.
  - Macro profiles (protein, calories, fiber) and educational explanations.

## Educational Guidelines

1. **Non-Curative**: Nutrition and lifestyle modifications are educational adjuncts to support metabolic health and insulin sensitivity. They are not medical cures.
2. **Inclusivity**: Accounts for regional dietary preferences:
   - Vegetarian
   - Eggitarian
   - Non-Vegetarian
   - Budget-conscious options featuring affordable pulses, millets, and seasonal greens.
